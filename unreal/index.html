Ashford Hollow

  • UE 5.8
  • C++
  • Gameplay Ability System
  • 2–4 players, server-authoritative

The Watchtower Guardian's slam telegraph. Its fill is synced to server time, so every client sees the same warning.

A medieval top-down pixel-art RPG slice for two to four players over a listen or dedicated server. The server decides every outcome; clients predict only what makes the game feel instant. Every gameplay system is C++, and art came from a CC0 pack.

Enemy AI

  • One AAHEnemyAIController with AI Perception (sight) and a behavior tree per enemy type, all sharing one Blackboard
  • Wolf pack: flank slots and per-target attack tokens, so only one wolf bites at a time
  • Bandit archer: an EQS query picks a firing spot that is in range, in sight and reachable; kiting falls out of that
  • Mini-boss: telegraphed slam and lane dash, with an observer abort that cuts the chase the moment an attack fits
  • NavMesh pathing with Detour crowd avoidance, so packs flow around each other

Custom behavior tree nodes

  • BTService_AHTrackTarget: writes perception results to the Blackboard (target, distance, line of sight)
  • BTTask_AHUseAbility: lets the tree fire Gameplay Abilities
  • BTTask_AHCircleTarget, BTDecorator_AHAttackToken, BTDecorator_AHTargetInRange with observer aborts

NPCs and quests

  • Quest giver NPC with an interaction prompt, typewriter dialog and per-player quest markers
  • Data-driven quests, a quest log per player and shared kill credit for the party

Lag compensation, measured

Studio work · Moshpit Studios

Moshpit virtual concert

  • UE5
  • AI programmer
  • Technical animator

Party rooms, the stadium crowd, Force Shot and Super Jump, and the in-game browser sending a video to the stage screens.

A multiplayer virtual concert. Players create or join a party room, then move through a stadium crowd of NPCs while video plays on the stage screens.

My part

  • NPC behavior with AI Controllers and behavior trees
  • Technical animation for the crowd and player characters
  • Testing the NPCs against the player powers

In the build

  • A dancing crowd that fills the floor and the stands
  • Player powers on cooldowns: Dash, Super Jump, Super Sprint and Force Shot, which knocks crowd NPCs into ragdoll
  • Party rooms you create, name and join
  • An in-game browser that puts a chosen video on the stage screens

Bamboo shrine and neon city levels: combat, elemental abilities and the skill tree.

A third-person action game across two levels, a bamboo shrine and a neon city. Melee combat with elemental abilities, enemies that hunt the player, and progression through XP and a skill tree.
